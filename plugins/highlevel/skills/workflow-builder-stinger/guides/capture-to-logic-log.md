# Capture to the Workflow Logic Log

Grounding: `references/research/distilled-research-article.md` section 5, from raw/04 (see the source table in the distillation).

Her workflow logic is still forming. This guide makes sure what she says while building is not lost.

## What counts as a capture

Capture when she says, in this conversation:
- **How she builds, names or sets up a workflow** ("I always split internal and client emails").
- **Why**, or corrects your approach with a reason ("no, use a tag here, because...").
- **A choice between options**, including one you offered. Log her choice, not your options or your reasoning.

Do not capture:
- Your own suggestions she only accepted ("sounds good", "yep"). Nothing she said is a rule yet.
- One-off facts about a single client's account.
- Anything already in the log with the same meaning.

When unsure whether it is a rule or a one-off, capture it. The monthly review sorts that out.

## Writing the entry

1. Read `Workflow Logic Log.md` fresh (see `references/vault-locations.md` for access).
2. Check for an existing entry with the same idea. Same idea: skip. Opposing idea: still add the new entry, and put the earlier entry's title and when each wins under `Tension`. Never edit or delete her earlier entries.
3. Build the entry in the page's exact format:

   ```
   #### YYYY-MM-DD · short title
   When: the situation
   Do: what Dani does
   Because: her reason
   Tension: competing idea, and when it wins (or "none")
   Source: [[MM-DD-YY]] or chat
   ```

   - Use today's date. The `·` separator is part of the format.
   - `Do` and `Because` use her words. Quote her directly when she gave a reason. No reason given: write `Because: (not said)`. Never invent one.
   - `Source`: `[[MM-DD-YY]]` when the work is happening from that day's note, otherwise `chat`.
   - No em dashes.
4. Insert it directly under the `### This month (...)` heading, above existing entries (newest first). Do not rename the heading or move entries: the monthly review does that.
5. Write the file back with the change-guard, touching nothing else.
6. Tell her in one line, after the main answer: `Logged to Workflow Logic Log: {short title}`.

## When writing is not possible

Print the entry in a code block under the answer with one line: `Couldn't reach your vault. Paste this under "This month" in Workflow Logic Log.`
